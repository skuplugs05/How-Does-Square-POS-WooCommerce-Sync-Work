# How-Does-Square-POS-WooCommerce-Sync-Work
[**Square POS WooCommerce Sync**](https://skuplugs.com/square-woocommerce-integration/) for SKUPlugs connects your physical retail store with your online WooCommerce store. It helps synchronize products, inventory, and order data between Square POS and WooCommerce. Businesses can reduce manual updates, minimize inventory discrepancies, and maintain accurate stock information across sales channels. SKUPlugs simplifies retail operations by creating a connected workflow between your POS and eCommerce store. This integration is useful for retailers seeking better inventory control, efficient order management, and smoother multichannel selling as their business grows.
